Our Story

Six Feet From The Water, One Flat-Top From Greatness.

Seaside Smashburger started as a single flat-top wheeled to the end of a pier — no menu, no plan, just a hot griddle and a hard press. What kept people walking back down that pier wasn't a gimmick. It was the crust.

We never grew the menu. We grew the line. Four builds, the same four sides, the same house sauce — just more people showing up before we've finished heating the griddle.

2019First cart, end of the pier
2021First stand, same four burgers
NowSame griddle. Same crust. Longer line.
